Why it matters

Google Maps stops at the gate.
We pick it up from there.

Static PDFs can't zoom, search or change with the event. Fans ask staff, guess, or give up. The digital map turns the stadium into something fans can search and follow, and gives our teams one source of truth for every location in the building.

Where we were

Static maps and scattered files

  • Top-down PDFs by level that fans can't search or zoom
  • Map files owned in different places with no version control
  • Concessions, activations and closures change faster than the maps do
  • Chatbot answers wayfinding in words only, with no visual route
  • Premium guests and first-timers rely on staff for directions
Where we're going

One living map, every channel

  • Searchable 3D map across levels, gates, food, restrooms and stores
  • Updates published from one dashboard and reflected everywhere
  • Event layouts for Falcons, Atlanta United, concerts and more
  • Chatbot hands fans a link that opens the exact spot on the map
  • Pre-built links that drop guests at their suite, gate or meeting point
The map and our AI chatbot work together, not against each other. When a fan's question needs visual wayfinding, the chatbot answers and links them straight to the right spot on the map.

Good to know

Quick answers.

Do fans need to download anything?

No. The map runs in any modern browser on phone or desktop and will be embedded on our website. Mappedin also has a mobile SDK so the same map can live inside our team and stadium apps.

Does it replace the chatbot?

No. The chatbot handles questions in words. The map handles orientation. When a fan asks something that needs a visual, the chatbot can link them right to the spot on the map. Satisfi can pull map images, links, categories and tags.

How fast can we update it?

Locations, hours and details are managed from one dashboard and pushed everywhere the map lives. Event versions sit on top of one base map, so switching between a Falcons game, an Atlanta United match or a concert is a configuration change, not a new map.

Is it accessible?

The interface is built for WCAG-aligned content, and directions can prioritize elevators and ramps. Accessible routing will matter most once Phase 2 wayfinding is live.

Will it work inside the stadium where GPS is weak?

Yes, a few ways. Fans can pick a starting point by hand, scan a “Start from Here” QR code, or use indoor positioning options Mappedin supports, including Apple's indoor positioning and Mappedin's visual positioning.

What will we learn from it?

Mappedin offers dashboards that show top searches, destinations, peak times and navigation patterns, plus raw data access through Snowflake. That tells us where fans get stuck and what they can't find.

What's still being refined?

The draft map is in QA. Recent fixes include rotating the stadium 180 degrees to match real orientation, adding gates, and checking concession accuracy, levels and seating. Feedback goes into a shared sheet that Mappedin works from.
